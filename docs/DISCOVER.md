## Feel statement (Technique 2 — sensory)
The product should feel like a **late-night printshop bench**: cold steel rulers on warm cream paper, the smell of ink, a single lamp cutting a hard edge across a poster. Quiet confidence — not a neon SaaS dashboard. Selecting a template should feel like sliding a screenprint frame into registration; exporting should feel like pulling a wet sheet off the press.

## Bold direction briefs (2–3)

### A — “Night Press” (ambitious pick)
Dark forge chrome, **paper-lit canvas well**, lime registration marks, Archivo wordmark, real ink-wash photography in empty states. AI assist as a **sidebar pressman** that rewrites copy to fit the fixed size. Closest to Snappa’s clarity (bright work surface) without copying their blue marketing site.

### B — “Gallery White” (discarded)
Full light UI, soft grey panels, sky accent. Too close to generic Canva/Snappa clones; loses Forge Ink identity; risks Inter-adjacent cleanliness.

### C — “Acid Poster” (discarded)
Full-bleed neon lime, poster-type only, no panels. Exciting as an art installation; fails the **composition job** (layers/properties become illegible).

## Pick
**A — Night Press.** Serves the one canvas job (contrast between tools and artboard), kills perma-dark slop, keeps a memorable forge identity.
